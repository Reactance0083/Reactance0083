I build practical AI workflow systems for businesses that need less manual rework, clearer handoffs, and better operating visibility.

## Current Focus: Independent Insurance Agency Operations

My current customer-facing product is **QuoteReady**, a validation-stage workflow for independent insurance agencies.

QuoteReady helps agency teams test how incomplete commercial intake can become a clearer submission packet with:

- a plain-language account summary
- a readiness score
- missing-information prompts
- underwriter concern flags
- producer follow-up questions
- beta and pilot interest paths

Live validation site: https://reactance0083.github.io/quoteready-validation/

QuoteReady is currently for validation and discovery. Use synthetic or fully redacted examples only. Do not submit client names, policy numbers, credentials, account numbers, private insured information, or unredacted policy files.

QuoteReady does not provide insurance, underwriting, coverage, legal, or compliance advice. Real submissions, coverage decisions, and pilot discussions should be reviewed by qualified humans.
